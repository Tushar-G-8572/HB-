Privacy Policy

HB+ Portal · Last updated: October 24, 2026

HB+ Portal is a small web and mobile application that lets people sign in with a Google account. Access is limited to users whose email address ends in @hbplus.fit. It was built as a demonstration project. This page explains what information the app uses.

Information we receive

When you choose “Login with Google”, Google shares the following basic profile information with the app:

  • Your email address
  • Your name
  • Your profile picture

The app does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

How we use it

  • To check that your email belongs to the allowed domain and decide whether you may sign in.
  • To show your name, email and picture on your dashboard.
  • To store your role (Admin or User) and your last login time, so access can be controlled.

If your email is not from the allowed domain, the sign-in is rejected and no account is created.

What is stored and where

Your email, name, picture, role and last login time are stored in a database hosted by Supabase. Your sign-in session (access and refresh tokens) is kept on your own device: in the secure storage of your phone, or in your browser’s local storage on the web. Logging out removes it from your device.

Sharing

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else. The services that process it on our behalf are Google (sign-in), Supabase (authentication and database) and Netlify (website hosting).
